Top Journal Article
The Case for Watching, Not Cutting

A new narrative review in Annals of Emergency Medicine walks through the four major RCTs (2,000+ adults, 2,000+ children) comparing antibiotics-first management against urgent appendectomy for uncomplicated appendicitis. The American College of Surgeons has endorsed nonoperative management as a safe alternative — though a separate meta-analysis found roughly 5x higher failure rates with the non-op route, so patient selection still matters at triage.

The "Human in the Loop" Illusion

Healthcare IT Today's Colin Hung talked with Julia Zarb of Blue x Blue about why standard IT guardrails don't hold up against agentic AI: errors that look perfectly logical in isolation can multiply fast once several AI agents pass data between each other across a health system. The uncomfortable takeaway — having "a human in the loop" somewhere doesn't automatically make an agentic workflow safe. Directly relevant to any physician-entrepreneur eyeing agentic AI tools for practice administration or a side business, not just health systems deploying it at scale.

Congress Races the Recess Clock

With the August 2026 recess looming, House committees advanced two transparency bills before breaking: H.R. 9393 (Lower Costs, More Transparency Act), requiring hospitals to post machine-readable files of standard charges, and H.R. 9504 (Tax-Exempt Hospital Transparency Act), which would require nonprofit hospitals to disclose more detail on IRS Form 990. Separately, a stopgap measure would hold federal spending at FY2026 levels through December 4, 2026.
