FDA Clears Aidoc's CT-Based AI Triage Platform — 14 Indications

The FDA granted 510(k) clearance to Aidoc's expanded CT-based AI triage software, now covering 14 cleared indications — flagging time-critical findings (large-vessel occlusion, pulmonary embolism, aortic dissection, and more) directly off the CT worklist before formal radiology read.

This is squarely relevant to an academic ED: triage-stage AI flagging on CT is one of the few AI categories with a plausible direct effect on door-to-treatment time for stroke and PE. Worth asking whether MetroHealth's imaging pipeline is evaluating it or a competitor product, since 14-indication breadth is now the bar competitors will be measured against.

DPC Hits an Inflection Point on HSA Rules

The direct primary care market is projected to grow from $70.17B in 2025 to $75.11B in 2026. The bigger story: IRS-finalized HR-1 rules now let patients pay DPC fees directly from an HSA, with a $150/month cap keeping the statutory line between DPC and concierge medicine intact — removing a major audit-risk objection that's held employers back.
