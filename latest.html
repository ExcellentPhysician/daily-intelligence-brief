Market Growth
DPC Hits an Inflection Point

The direct primary care market is on track to grow from $70.17B (2025) to $75.11B in 2026 — a 7.0% CAGR — per Medical Economics' coverage of the Hint Health 2026 Trends Report. DPC membership is up 837% since 2017, accelerated by the January 2026 rule letting HSA funds pay DPC membership fees (capped at $150/month) under the OBBBA.

Not everyone's convinced the access problem is solved — see KevinMD's counterpoint that the HSA fix mainly benefits patients who already have HSA-eligible plans.

State-Level Model
Oklahoma's HCSM Tax Break Points the Way

Oklahoma's Health Care Sharing Ministries Tax Parity Act takes effect November 1, 2026, letting members deduct HCSM membership costs starting tax year 2027 and exempting qualified health-care shares from taxable income. With roughly 1.7 million Americans in HCSMs nationally, this could become a template other states copy.
