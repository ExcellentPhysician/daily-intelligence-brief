A Phone Call That Screens for Cognitive Decline

At the Alzheimer's Association International Conference (AAIC) 2026 in London today, CaringAI presented data on CaringAI Listen, a conversational voice agent that runs a validated cognitive assessment over an ordinary phone call — no app, device, or setup required — then generates a DSM-5-aligned report.

In the study, a certified dementia practitioner accepted the AI's scoring and triage without modification in roughly 80% of 68 reviewed assessments. For an ED physician, it's a preview of how passive, phone-based AI screening could extend specialist-level triage into primary-care and community settings that have no dementia specialist at all — the same access gap driving ARPA-H's new agentic-AI cardiology program, which is racing to build the first FDA-authorized clinical AI agent for 24/7 heart-failure management.

HSA Dollars Can Now Pay for DPC Memberships

IRS-finalized rules under HR 1 now permit HSA funds to pay DPC membership fees, with a $150/month cap that keeps DPC statutorily distinct from concierge medicine. HRAs May Soon Reimburse HCSM Payments Tax-Free

A proposed IRS rule would let Health Reimbursement Arrangements (HRAs) reimburse employees' monthly health-sharing-ministry payments tax-free, treating HCSM "shares" the same way as DPC fees and certain public-coverage payments — a meaningful employer-side lever for the roughly one million Americans currently using health sharing.
