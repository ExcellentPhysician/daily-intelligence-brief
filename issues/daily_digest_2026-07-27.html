New Income Idea
Sell Covered Calls on the MSTR Shares You Already Hold

You already own MSTR — you're not currently generating income from that position beyond price appreciation. Selling out-of-the-money covered calls (30–45 days to expiration) against existing shares converts MSTR's notoriously high implied volatility into recurring premium income, without adding new capital or new conviction risk.

Why it fits: zero new capital required, uses an asset you already believe in, and MSTR's elevated IV (it moves ~3x BTC) means call premiums run rich compared to most equities.

Income range: highly position-size dependent — roughly $200–$1,500/month per 100 shares depending on strike distance and IV at the time, with the tradeoff of capping upside if MSTR rips.

Start today: enable options trading (Level 1 covered calls) on your existing brokerage — Fidelity or Robinhood both support it — then sell your first 30-day OTM call against a portion of your position.

Friction: requires options approval (same-day for most brokerages), and caps upside on shares called away — size it against only part of your position.
