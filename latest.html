FDA Clears Bedside Cerebral Fluid Monitor — STEDI Device

NeuraNova Inc. received FDA 510(k) clearance today for its STEDI Device, a bioimpedance spectroscopy monitor that measures impedance ratios between cerebral hemispheres to flag fluid-volume asymmetry — a bedside, non-invasive signal relevant to stroke and traumatic brain injury triage.

Why it matters for an academic EM physician: this is squarely in the "point-of-care neuro assessment" lane POCUS already occupies for the abdomen and heart — a plausible future adjunct for early stroke/TBI screening before CT, and a case study in how narrow-indication bioimpedance devices are clearing FDA faster than broad AI diagnostic software right now.

Same day: Momentis Surgical cleared a multiport robotic surgical platform and Neko Health (Daniel Ek's preventative-scanning venture) closed a $700M Series C — a sign capital is rotating toward deep diagnostic tech over consumer wellness apps.
